# Derived metrics use published methods and carry a disclaimer

Where Garmin does not provide a metric, the app computes it from a published scientific method rather than inventing one. Each derived metric cites its method and states its limits. Provenance is shown quietly (a small note per metric), and a clear disclaimer says computed values are estimates, are not Garmin's numbers and are not medical advice. This replaces the earlier rule of labelling every computed number prominently, which crowded the screen. Impact views also say they show association, not cause.
