How is this scored?

Readiness compares last night's HRV, resting heart rate, sleep time and Body Battery peak with your own previous 28 days. 60 means typical for you, and each standard deviation from your norm moves it 20 points. Under 30 is strained, 30 to 49 below usual, 50 to 69 typical, 70 and up well recovered. It is a simple guide computed here, not a Garmin metric and not medical advice.

The load ratio is your last 7 days of training load divided by your usual week (28-day total divided by 4). Under 0.8 is lighter than usual, up to 1.3 in your sweet spot, up to 1.5 ramping up, above 1.5 spiking.

What is training load?

Training load is the score Garmin gives each activity for how demanding it was, added up over a week. The ratio divides your last 7 days by your usual week (your 28-day total divided by 4). Under 0.8 is lighter than usual, up to 1.3 your sweet spot, up to 1.5 ramping up, and above that spiking. It describes your own recent pattern, not a limit, and it is not medical advice.
